Paper Flowers

Follow these simple steps to create stunning paper flowers:

  • Step 1: Gather colored paper, scissors, and glue.
  • Step 2: Cut the paper into petal shapes.
  • Step 3: Glue the petals together in layers.
  • Step 4: Add a center piece and let it dry.

These flowers make a great decoration for any occasion!

Macramé Wall Hanging

Learn how to create a macramé wall hanging:

  • Step 1: Cut three long strands of rope.
  • Step 2: Begin knotting the ropes into simple patterns.
  • Step 3: Attach to a wooden dowel or stick for hanging.
  • Step 4: Finish with fringe or other decorative elements.

This craft is perfect for personalizing your living space!
